## Design work

Before designing, generating, or laying out any UI for this product — in
code, in Paper, in Figma, anywhere — read `REFERENCE.md` and `DESIGN.md` in
full. Every color, size, and spacing value comes from those files. If
something you're asked to design isn't covered there, ask rather than
choosing on your own. When finished, check the output against both files,
fix what doesn't match, and only then show it.
